---- What it is ----
Chess 47 lets you play chess against your calculator. The board is drawn on the screen and you move the pieces
with the number keys. The C47 knows all the rules (castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate,
draw by repetition or by too little material), opens from a small book of known openings and then thinks two
moves ahead. It can also play against itself.

---- How to use it ----
Load CHESS47 and press XEQ CHESS47. Setting up takes about 10 seconds. You have the white pieces and it is your move.
  8 2 4 6   move the cursor up, down, left and right (as you see the board)
  5         pick up the piece under the cursor (it shows white on black); move the cursor to the square where it
            goes and press 5 again. 5 on the same square puts it back. An illegal move is refused.
  +         swap sides: the C47 takes the side to move and you take the other. Press it at the start to play
            black; the board turns so that black is at the bottom.
  9         C47 vs C47: the calculator plays both sides until checkmate or a draw. Press any key to stop it and
            take over the side to move.
  3         two players: two people play on the calculator; the C47 only checks that every move is legal and
            calls check, checkmate and the draws (+ or 9 give it a side again)
  7         a new game
  1         the level: 2 (two moves ahead, about 10-20 s a move), 1 (one move ahead, a few seconds), 0 CRAZY
            (random legal moves, with a crazy face by the title)
  0         the end. A game that is not over is kept: the next XEQ CHESS47 goes on where you left it.
The right side shows your side and level, whose move it is (CHECK, CHECKMATE ... when it happens) and the last
seven moves. The clock at the top left shows each side's time left: 5 minutes each and 10 seconds more for every
move played. Only a person's clock runs (the C47's stops while it thinks, so it never loses on time). A person
whose time runs out loses (WHITE / BLACK LOST ON TIME), unless the opponent could not mate by any series of legal
moves (a bare king, for example): then it is a draw (DRAW: TIME, NO MATE).
Promotion is always to a queen. There is no 50-move rule.

---- What it changes on your calculator ----
Your stack and LASTx are given back at the end. Registers R00-R19 are not touched; R20-R99 are used and cleared
at the end. While it runs it makes the matrices BD, GL, GH, OB and the variables TK, TW, TB (the time left), NW, NB; at the end
it deletes them, except the saved game: the variable CHN with GH, TW and TB (they stay until the game is over or
you start a new one). It works with short integers in UNSIGN mode and gives you back your own integer mode, then
sets WSIZE 64, GRMOD 0 and GRFNT 20.

---- Programs and labels ----
One program, CHESS47, with one global label (CHESS47). Every routine inside is a local label (LBL 01-99), so
nothing else is added to your program catalogue. It uses the STRUCT commands (IF / ELSE / ENDIF, DO / WHILE /
ENDDO) of the C47 / R47 firmware, ATEXT and GRFNT, AGRAPH and the bitwise commands AND OR XOR NOT BS?.
