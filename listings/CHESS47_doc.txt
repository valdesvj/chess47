      ; CHESS47 v0.2.0 - Chess 47: a chess engine for the C47 / R47 (and the DM42n with the C47 firmware)
      ; You play the white pieces against the C47 (+ hands your side to the C47: it plays the side to move).
      ; The rules: every legal move, castling (not out of, through or into check), en passant, promotion (always to a
      ; queen); check, checkmate and stalemate. No draw by repetition or by the 50 moves.
      ; The engine (a port of the matrix "mini engine" idea of Micro-Max, H.G. Muller): the board is the 10 x 12 mailbox
      ; of the row 1 of the matrix BD (column s = 10 row + col + 1: a1 22 ... h1 29 ... a8 92 ... h8 99, the border 8); a move
      ; is from x 1000 + to; the score SC (material + piece-square, white positive) is kept up to date by each move from
      ; the table PT (row piece + 11, column the square: the signed value of that piece there); the moves of a ply go
      ; in a row ML, the undo data in a row UN. Board, ML, PT and UN are the rows of ONE matrix BD (22 x 120: row 1 the
      ; board, 2-4 the move lists of the root, the replies, the moves played; 5-17 PT; 18-20 UN; 21 the knight masks, 22 the square bits): one INDEX, no switching. The search: 2 plies (level 2) or 1, alpha-beta
      ; cuts, the captures first; python/chess47ref.py is the same algorithm in Python (the tests compare the two).
      ; The 64-bit bitwise commands: the castling rights are a 4-bit word (1 white short, 2 white long, 4 black short,
      ; 8 black long: AND clears a right, BS? tests one); the dark squares are worked out with AND, NOT and OR from the
      ; glyph words (dither AND NOT mask OR glyph), WSIZE 24 while drawing: a square is 24 rows, one AGRAPH word a column;
      ; the knights are two 64-bit words (WSIZE 64 the rest of the time) for the attack test.
      ; Keys: 8 2 4 6 the cursor up, down, left, right; 5 picks a piece of yours, then 5 on the square to play it (5 on
      ; the same square drops it); + the C47 plays the side to move (you take the other side); 1 the level 2 -> 1 -> 0
      ; (CRAZY: random legal moves, a crazy face by the title);
      ; 0 ends. The C47 thinks about 15-30 s at level 2 (the simulator's step count), 1-3 s at level 1.
      ; The user's stack and LASTx are kept (LocR 9: STOS R.00, LASTx in R.08); R00-R19 are not touched; at the end
      ; R20-R99 are cleared, the matrices BD GL GH OB deleted, WSIZE 64, GRMOD 0 and GRFNT 20 set.
      ; Registers: R20 the side to move (+1 white, -1 black), R21 the en passant square (0 none), R22 the castling word,
      ; R23 SC, R24 R25 the white and black king's squares, R26 the level, R27 your side, R28 the cursor, R29 the
      ; square picked (0 none); R30-R39 the move generator (R30 the square, R31 its piece x its colour, R32 the colour,
      ; R33 the target, R34 the direction, R35 R36 the first and the end offset register, R37 the moves, R38 the row
      ; of ML, R39 1 with castling); R40-R49 make / unmake (R40 the move, R41 from, R42 to, R43 the piece, R44 the
      ; piece taken, R45 its colour, R46 the ply: the row of UN, R47 1 en passant / 2 castling, R48 R49 work);
      ; R50-R56 attacked? (R50 the square, R51 the colour of the attackers, R52 the target, R53 the direction, R54 its
      ; register, R55 the answer, R56 work); R57-R59 the castling word before the move of ply 1-3; R60-R71 the
      ; search (R60 its side, R61 the best value, R62 the best move, R63 R64 the root move and count, R65 the value,
      ; R66 R67 the reply and count, R68 R69 the pass (1 the captures, 0 the others), R70 the cut, R71 a reply's
      ; value); R72 the quiet moves of a generation; R73-R79 the last 7 moves (texts, R79 the newest); R80-R87 the knight's offsets, R88-R95 the
      ; directions (R88-R91 the diagonals, R92-R95 the lines); R96 the move number, R97 1 when the game is over, R98 the
      ; key, R99 an AGRAPH word. Drawing reuses R30-R36 and R48 R49.
      ; Screen 400 x 240, row 0 = bottom: the board at x 12-203, rows 20-211 (python/layout.py); the panel at x 212.
      ; Control flow in the C47 STRUCT commands (IF / ELSE / ENDIF, DO / WHILE / ENDDO), indented two spaces per
